## What Is Unity?

Unity is a principle of persuasion in which people are more likely to be influenced by individuals or groups with whom they share a sense of identity or belonging.

Unlike the Liking principle, which focuses on personal attraction, familiarity, and positive relationships, Unity emphasizes a deeper sense of shared identity. People may be more receptive to messages from those they consider part of their own group.

Shared identity can develop through common experiences, communities, cultural backgrounds, or collective goals.

## How Is Unity Used in Web Design?

Websites can apply the Unity principle by creating a sense of community and emphasizing shared interests or goals among users.

Common techniques include community discussion spaces, membership groups, shared challenges, collaborative projects, and messaging that highlights collective identity.

When users feel that they belong to a community, they may be more motivated to participate, contribute, and remain engaged with a website or application.

## Example 1: Online Gaming Community

An online gaming platform could create communities where players join teams, participate in group challenges, and work toward shared achievements.

For example, a website might invite players to join a guild and collaborate to unlock rewards.

Players who identify with their group may be more willing to participate because their actions contribute to the success of the community
