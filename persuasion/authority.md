## What Is Authority?

Authority is a principle of persuasion in which people are more likely to trust and follow recommendations from individuals or organizations they perceive as knowledgeable, experienced, or credible.

People often rely on experts to help make decisions, especially when they lack the knowledge or experience to evaluate something themselves.

## How Is Authority Used in Web Design?

Websites use authority to establish credibility and encourage visitors to trust their information, products, or services.

Common examples include displaying professional credentials, expert endorsements, certifications, awards, and references to reputable organizations.

Authority can be especially influential on websites involving healthcare, education, finance, or other subjects where specialized knowledge is important.

## Example 1: Healthcare Website

A healthcare website could display the qualifications and professional experience of its doctors alongside their medical advice.

For example, an article about nutrition might include the author's medical credentials and professional background.

Visitors may be more likely to trust the information because it comes from someone with relevant expertise.

## Example 2: Online Learning Platform

An educational website could highlight the academic qualifications and professional achievements of its instructors.

For example, a programming course might feature an instructor with extensive software development experience or a relevant university degree.

These credentials can help prospective students feel more confident about enrolling in the course.

## Ethical Considerations

Authority should be represented accurately. Websites should not invent professional qualifications, exaggerate expertise, or imply endorsements that do not exist.

Designers should also make it possible for users to verify credentials and evaluate the quality of information independently.
